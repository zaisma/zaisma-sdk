# Security policy

Found a security hole in Zaisma? Tell us privately, and you won't get in trouble for looking. The full policy is at [zaisma.com/legal/security](https://zaisma.com/legal/security).

## How to report

Email [security@zaisma.com](mailto:security@zaisma.com), or [report it privately on GitHub](https://github.com/zaisma/zaisma-sdk/security/advisories/new). Say where it is, how to make it happen, and what it lets someone do. Please don't open a public issue or pull request for a hole.

## What's covered

- zaisma.com, its API and its MCP.
- Your own Zaisma account and your own games.
- The game server, the SDK and the upload command in this repo.

The rules, safe harbor and what to expect are in the full policy.
